Visual Direction & Design System

A digital marketing agency needs to look like it practices what it preaches. I built a sharp, modern visual identity, bold typography, a high-contrast palette, and a design system that signals expertise at first scroll.

Every component was designed to position Digiseo as the obvious authority in the room. The visual language is confident, not flashy, the kind of agency a serious business director trusts to handle their digital presence.

Conversion-Focused Layout

The layout follows a single B2B journey: discover → evaluate → contact. Services are broken down with clear outcomes, not just feature lists. Case results and social proof appear exactly where a prospect's doubt would otherwise form.

The contact flow is streamlined and low-friction. A persistent CTA follows the scroll on every page, always visible, never intrusive. The architecture converts qualified visitors without a single unnecessary click.
